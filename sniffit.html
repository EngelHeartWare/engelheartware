Research & citation

Sniff It Version 1 explores a personal smell hub architecture: olfactory training, personal scent organization, everyday smell journaling, and longitudinal self-observation are connected in one user-controlled system. It is not presented as a validated clinical intervention.

Research status V1 Beta · validation ongoing

Sniff It Version 1 is documented in a published system-design preprint. A prospective feasibility and usability study is being prepared to examine initial usability, intended use, and four weeks of self-directed real-world use. Sniff It is not presented as a clinically validated intervention, and app-based ratings and home activities are personal records, not substitutes for standardized psychophysical olfactory testing.

Design boundaries

Sniff It separates subjective experience from clinical interpretation. AI may assist with capture, language, and organization, while safety rules and longitudinal calculations remain deterministic and auditable.

Smart Scent Capture Beta
Recognizes visible objects or labels and proposes a catalog match where available; otherwise, the user can confirm or edit the detected name and add it as a custom scent. It cannot smell a photograph and the user confirms the result.

AI Scent Moment suggestions Beta
Optional suggestions for titles, mentioned smells, and tags after the user has written their own note.

The idea

One place for your sense of smell.

Food. Memories. Places. Emotion. Safety.

Smell training is only one part of how we experience smell. Sniff It connects deliberate training with the scents you keep, the moments you notice, and the observations you build over time.

The result is a personal smell hub: one place to train, organize, notice, record, learn, and revisit your own history—without turning subjective experience into a clinical score.

Structured olfactory training has been studied across several forms of olfactory dysfunction. Sniff It Version 1 itself has not yet been shown to improve adherence or clinical outcomes.

Version 1

One hub, connected around you

Rather than treating training as a standalone timer, Sniff It connects training, My Scents, Scent Moments, statistics, learning, and export around one personal smell history.

Structured olfactory training

Choose exactly four scents from My Scents and use the same guided training flow for first and later sessions. Timed exposure, personal ratings, reminders, streaks, and achievements support regular practice.

Personal Scent Library

Add familiar scents manually, from the curated catalog, or with Smart Scent Capture. A newly introduced scent receives a personal first impression before reference descriptors are revealed, reducing priming.

Scent Moments

Record spontaneous smell experiences with a title, free-text note, tags, and optional photo or location. These are contextual journal entries rather than diagnostic symptom questionnaires.

Longitudinal statistics

Review training activity, personal ratings, descriptor histories, and scent-specific observations. Changes are shown as recorded patterns, not interpreted as recovery or deterioration.

Learn this scent

Optional educational exercises compare the user’s own words with reference vocabulary after an independent observation. There is no correctness score.

Export and optional integrations

User-controlled CSV and PDF export are available. Optional HealthKit writing, photos, location, notifications, and widgets remain under the user’s control.

Privacy

Local by design.

Sniff It does not require an account and does not automatically send personal smell records to a research backend. App data is stored locally on the device. Sharing happens only when the user explicitly exports or shares data. Optional system features such as HealthKit, photos, location, notifications, and widgets use their respective Apple permissions.

Common questions

Is Sniff It a medical device or diagnostic test?

No. Sniff It records training activity and subjective observations. It does not diagnose olfactory disorders or replace standardized clinical smell testing.

Does the camera “smell” an object?

No. Smart Scent Capture Beta uses visible image and text cues to identify an object or label and suggest a catalog match where available. If there is no exact catalog match, the user can confirm or edit the detected name and add it as a custom scent. The user confirms the result, and safety decisions remain rule-based.

Do I need a commercial smell-training kit?

No. Users can organize safe, familiar scent sources they already have and select four for training. Product and material safety instructions still apply.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on the device. Sniff It has no automatic research upload. Users can explicitly export CSV or PDF files when they choose.

Is there a subscription?

No subscription is planned for Version 1. Optional Pro and Supporter upgrades are one-time purchases; the core training experience remains usable without a subscription.

What is being evaluated next?

A prospective feasibility and usability study is being prepared to examine initial usability, intended use, and self-directed use over four weeks, including natural training adherence among users who intend to train. Later studies may use standardized olfactory outcomes.
