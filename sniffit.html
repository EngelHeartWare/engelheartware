Privacy

Your smell history belongs to you.

Sniff It does not require an account and does not automatically send personal smell records to a research backend. App data is stored locally on your iPhone; sharing happens only when you choose to export or share it.

  • No account required
  • No advertising
  • No automatic research upload
  • Export initiated by you

Why Sniff It may be useful

Version 1 brings several parts of the participant experience into one bilingual iOS app:

  • structured smell training and self-reported session history;
  • everyday scent journaling alongside deliberate practice;
  • a personal scent library and scent-specific observations;
  • user-controlled PDF and CSV exports;
  • explicit separation of subjective records from clinical measurement.

Good to know

Common questions

Is Sniff It a medical device or diagnostic test?

No. Sniff It supports personal smell training and records subjective observations. It does not diagnose smell disorders, measure olfactory function, or replace standardized clinical testing or professional medical care.

Do I need a commercial smell-training kit?

No. You can organize safe, familiar scent sources you already have and choose four for training. Always follow product and material safety instructions; do not deliberately smell unknown substances, smoke, solvents, or strong irritants.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your device. Sniff It has no automatic research upload. You can explicitly create and share a PDF or CSV export when you choose.

Is there a subscription?

No subscription is planned for Version 1. Core training, journaling, statistics, and export remain usable without one. Optional Supporter purchases, if offered, are limited to cosmetic extras.

How can I discuss a research project or provide professional feedback?

Email neurosense@fau.de. Questions about methods, usability, collaboration, or whether Sniff It could be useful to the people you support are welcome.
