Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What does Skyline Explorer collect?

Nothing. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking libraries. I never see which buildings you look at or which ones you mark as visited.

What is stored on your device?

  • Which buildings you've marked as visited, favorited or wishlisted, and the date of each visit
  • Any note you write about a visit, and any photo of your own you attach to it
  • Which building was picked as Skyscraper of the Day today
  • A cache of the photos and descriptions you've already viewed

This is stored with Apple's SwiftData and UserDefaults, in the app's own container; your own photos are saved as ordinary files alongside it. All of it is included in your normal iPhone backup and is deleted when you delete the app. Choosing a photo uses Apple's system picker, which hands the app that one image and gives it no access to the rest of your photo library.

What leaves your device?

The building facts — heights, floors, years, coordinates — ship inside the app, so browsing and the map work with no connection at all. Photos and the descriptive texts are fetched from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons when you open a building's card, which means Wikimedia's servers see the request the way they would if you opened the article in Safari. No identifier of you or your device is attached, and the results are cached so the same building isn't fetched twice.

Maps

The map is Apple's MapKit. Map data is requested from Apple as you pan and zoom, under Apple's privacy policy. The app does not request your location and cannot see where you are.

The Skyline Pro purchase

Skyline Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. Payment details go to Apple, never to me, and I receive no personal data from the transaction — only the anonymous, aggregated sales figures every developer sees.

Children's privacy

Skyline Explorer collects no data from any user, including children under 13.

Common questions

Do I need Pro to use the app?

No. Every building, the map, search and the Skyscraper of the Day are free, along with your first 10 visit stamps. Pro adds the 3D city and unlimited stamps.

Is it a subscription?

No. Skyline Pro is a single €4.99 purchase. There is no subscription in this app and there isn't going to be one.

Does it work offline?

The buildings, the list, the search and your Travel Book do. Photos and descriptions need a connection the first time you open a building — after that they're cached.

Where does the data come from?

Facts from Wikidata, descriptions from Wikipedia, photos from Wikimedia Commons — each credited in the app with its author and licence. See the credits below.

Can I stamp a building that isn't finished?

No — more than 120 of the towers are still planned or under construction, and you can browse them, but the stamp only unlocks once a building actually exists.

What iOS version do I need?

iOS 18 or later, on iPhone and iPad.
