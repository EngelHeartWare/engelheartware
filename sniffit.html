The Idea

Smell is part of everyday life.

Food. Memories. Places. Emotions. Safety.

For some people, their sense of smell changes or disappears. For others, smell is simply a sense they want to notice more consciously.

Sniff It gives you a simple, beautiful way to pay attention, practice regularly, and keep a lasting record of your progress.

(Structured olfactory training is also used in clinical rehabilitation for smell loss. Sniff It itself, however, is not a medical device.)

Step 1

Train your nose

Run structured, timed sessions with your chosen familiar scents. Challenge your perception and rate the intensity and details of what you experience.

Privacy

Your data stays yours.

Sniff It has no analytics, no ads, and no account requirements. Everything you log lives securely on your device and syncs only through your own private iCloud via Apple CloudKit. Neither the developer nor any third party can see it.

Common questions

Who is Sniff It for?

Sniff It is for anyone interested in paying more attention to their sense of smell. It can also support people who are practising structured smell training after experiencing smell loss.

Do I need special equipment?

No special hardware is required. You can use familiar scents you already have at home (like coffee, spices, or essential oils) and let Sniff It guide and record your sessions.

Is Sniff It really free?

Yes. The full training routine is completely ad-free and usable without any purchases.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and in your personal iCloud via Apple CloudKit. The developer has no access to it at any time.
