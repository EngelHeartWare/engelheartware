Privacy

Your records stay on your iPhone.

Sniff It does not require an account. The app stores your records locally and does not upload them to a research server. You decide when to create or share an export.

  • No account required
  • No advertising
  • No automatic data transfer
  • PDF and CSV export

Common questions

Is Sniff It a medical device or diagnostic test?

No. Sniff It supports personal smell training and records subjective observations. It does not diagnose smell disorders, measure olfactory function, or replace standardized clinical testing or professional medical care.

Do I need a commercial smell-training kit?

No. You can organize safe, familiar scent sources you already have and choose four for training. Always follow product and material safety instructions; do not deliberately smell unknown substances, smoke, solvents, or strong irritants.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your device. Sniff It does not send your records to a research server automatically. You can create and share a PDF or CSV export when you choose.

Is there a subscription?

No subscription is planned for Version 1. Core training, journaling, statistics, and export remain usable without one. Optional Supporter purchases, if offered, are limited to cosmetic extras.
